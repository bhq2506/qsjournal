How journals are assigned. A journal appears under a QS subject when at least one of its Scopus ASJC codes matches the ASJC codes QS maps to that subject. A journal with several ASJC codes can sit in several subjects.

About the quartile. Quartile is the journal's SJR 2025 best quartile across all its Scopus categories. It is not specific to the QS subject shown, so a journal marked Q1 may be Q2 or lower in the category that matches your subject. Check the Scopus page before relying on it.
