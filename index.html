Only the key phrases extracted from your text are sent to OpenAlex; the full abstract stays in your browser. Results are suggestions: always read the journal's aims and scope before submitting.

How it works
  1. Key phrases are taken from your title, abstract and keywords.
  2. OpenAlex is searched for similar articles published in the last six years.
  3. Journals are ranked by how many similar articles they published and how closely those match, limited to the Scopus journals in this list.

How journals are assigned. A journal appears under a QS subject when at least one of its Scopus ASJC codes matches the ASJC codes QS maps to that subject. A journal with several ASJC codes can sit in several subjects.

Subject area classification. QS broad faculty areas, subject areas and their ASJC codes follow QS Support: All Science Journal Classifications (ASJC) Codes.

About the quartile. Quartile is the journal's SJR 2025 best quartile across all its Scopus categories. It is not specific to the QS subject shown, so a journal marked Q1 may be Q2 or lower in the category that matches your subject. Check the Scopus page before relying on it.
